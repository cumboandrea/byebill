Privacy Policy

ByeBill · Subscription management app

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Data Controller

Andrea Cumbo (independent developer / sole trader), contact email hola@byebill.eu, is the data controller in connection with the ByeBill app available on the Google Play Store.

2. Summary: privacy by design

ByeBill does not collect personal data. All of your data (subscriptions, amounts, billing dates, preferences and settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. The app has no user accounts, uses no analytics of our own, and does not share your data with third parties except as described in this policy.

3. Involved third parties

3.1. Google Play (distribution and purchases)

The app is distributed through the Google Play Store. If you purchase the Premium plan (monthly, yearly or lifetime), payments are handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes payment data under its own Privacy Policy, over which we have no control.

3.2. Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version of ByeBill shows advertising served by Google AdMob. Google may process data (such as advertising identifiers) to display and measure ads. Personalized ads are shown only if you give your consent through the consent form that appears on first use. You can manage or withdraw your consent at any time from the app's Settings. Without consent, only non-personalized ads (or none) are shown.

4. Legal basis for processing

  • Personalized advertising: your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Local app data: not processed by us; it lives on your device (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in a good user experience).

5. Retention and deletion

Since we do not collect personal data, no retention period on servers applies. You can delete all of your data with the "Reset data" button in Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Calendar events created by the app can be removed from Settings.

6. Minors

The app is not directed at children under 16 and does not collect data from minors.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability and object. To exercise them, write to hola@byebill.eu. You may also file a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD at www.aepd.es).

8. Changes to this policy

We will notify you of changes by updating the date above. Substantial changes to processing will be announced within the app.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions: hola@byebill.eu.
